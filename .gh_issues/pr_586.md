## Description

This PR introduces the Automated Global Tax Engine & Compliance Calculator (Issue #586).

Currently, PaySphere relies on manual deduction inputs to calculate payroll. This approach fails to scale globally as tax rules, brackets, and social security deductions change radically across jurisdictions (e.g., US vs UK vs India). 

This PR implements a dynamic calculation engine to automate this compliance:
- **`TaxBracket` Schema**: A robust model to store regional tax configurations, supporting multiple dynamic brackets (`minIncome`, `maxIncome`, `ratePercentage`, `fixedDeduction`) and a base `socialSecurityRate`.
- **`TaxService` Engine**: A stateless mathematical engine that takes gross annual income and a region code, and automatically recursively steps through the brackets to compute the total tax burden, effective tax rate, and social security deductions.
- **Controller Injection**: Integrates the `TaxService` directly into the `generatePayroll` controller pipeline, acting as the foundation to dynamically fetch regional configurations and automatically inject `deductions` before finalizing net salary.
